### Does EvAGI solve catastrophic forgetting?

No. The reported zero-forgetting result comes from allocating disjoint parameters per task. That removes interference structurally; it does not improve how a model retains knowledge.

### How many runs are published?

Two: a 17K-parameter system on a twenty-shape task, and a 33M-parameter language model on five sequential skills plus live facts.

### Are the results reproducible?

No. Nothing is released. There are no seeds, repeats or error bars to replicate against.

### What is the capacity law?

A measured relationship between the number of active parameters and resulting accuracy, used to predict how much capacity a task needs.

### Is EvAGI the same as EvMind?

No. EvMind is the research programme; EvAGI is the experimental system the programme is measured on.

### How is this different from EvAgent?

EvAgent orchestrates software-development work. EvAGI studies how a model should allocate and retain capability.
